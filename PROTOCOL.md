# Protocol: GAHT TrialScope

## Research Question
What registered studies on ClinicalTrials.gov explicitly investigate gender-affirming hormone therapy among transgender and gender-diverse people, and how are those studies distributed by registration year, study design, recruitment status, hormone intervention or exposure, participant age group, enrollment, sponsor, geographic location, and results availability?

## Intended Use
This project maps the registered research landscape. It does **not** evaluate whether GAHT is safe, effective, clinically appropriate, or superior; determine appropriate hormone levels or doses; provide medical advice; or claim to represent all published GAHT research. ClinicalTrials.gov is a registry and does not represent all GAHT research.

## Inclusion Criteria
A study is potentially includable only when BOTH conditions are met:
1. Transgender, transsexual, nonbinary, gender-diverse, gender-incongruent, or another clearly transgender/gender-diverse population is explicitly part of the study population.
2. Gender-affirming hormone therapy is an explicit:
   - intervention,
   - exposure,
   - comparison,
   - monitoring target,
   - pharmacologic variable, or
   - subject of analysis.

Examples: studies of estradiol, estrogen, testosterone, antiandrogens, spironolactone, progesterone, hormone concentrations, GAHT pharmacokinetics, dosing approaches, monitoring strategies, or health outcomes explicitly analyzed in relation to GAHT.

## Exclusion Criteria
Exclude:
- Hormone studies unrelated to transgender/gender-diverse populations.
- Hormones used for non-GAHT indications (menopause, hypogonadism, contraception, fertility treatment, cancer therapy).
- Transgender-health studies where hormone use is merely incidental, demographic, or background information.
- Gender-affirming care studies with no hormone-specific research component.
- Puberty-suppression-only studies using GnRH agonists with no explicit estradiol/testosterone GAHT component.

## Scope
Include candidates regardless of age group, country, registration year, recruitment status, interventional versus observational design, or whether ClinicalTrials.gov results are posted. Do not restrict the search to U.S. studies.

## Screening States
Search terms identify candidate studies only. Preliminary AI-assisted screening states are:
- `include`: Strong evidence that both inclusion criteria are met.
- `exclude`: Strong evidence that the study meets exclusion criteria.
- `uncertain`: Ambiguous records needing human review.

These are AI-assisted preliminary screening decisions only and must not be represented as human-verified decisions.
